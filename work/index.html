OpenClaw & Alfred

A community innovation space was managing event inquiries, member communications, proposal generation, and facility scheduling across email, spreadsheets, and group texts. Staff spent more time routing information than serving members.

We designed and deployed OpenClaw — a multi-agent AI system that handles inquiry intake, workflow routing, and proposal generation end to end. Alfred orchestrates. A Monitor agent runs local Qwen inference. A Drafting agent generates proposals on Claude Sonnet. The system integrates across WhatsApp, email, and internal tools, with human oversight at every decision point.

Built on a Mac Mini M4. Google Drive as system of record. Three agents in production, zero new tools for staff to learn. Response time went from hours to under two minutes.

Esports Seed Round
